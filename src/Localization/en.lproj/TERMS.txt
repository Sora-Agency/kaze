3. YOUR RESPONSIBILITY WHEN USING THE APP
You are responsible for deciding what to scan, clean, uninstall or delete, reviewing selections and maintaining adequate backups of your files and settings. Only act on data and devices you are authorized to manage. System permissions alone do not establish that authorization.

Moving an app or its data to the Trash may end sessions, lose settings, affect projects or prevent an app from working. Kaze does not automatically empty the Trash, but being able to restore an item does not guarantee restoration of the app's previous state.

Selected Docker and Xcode resources are removed using their respective tools and may be permanently deleted without going through the Trash. Volumes may contain databases. Running containers may be stopped. Deleted caches may require new downloads, builds or installations. Review the confirmations shown by the app.

Scheduled cleanup is preset for 09:30 and disabled. You can create multiple independent tasks with their own times, conditions and cleanup categories in Scheduled Tasks, disable them there or remove them with the uninstaller. Updating from an old version disables its default schedule; later updates preserve schedules you explicitly enabled. Scheduled cleanup does not use explorer selections to delete documents.

4. SIZES, PERMISSIONS AND LIMITATIONS
Sizes reflect metadata and allocated disk space during the scan. They do not guarantee how much space will be recovered after deletion. APFS, shared files, links, compression, snapshots, cloud files and changes during a scan may produce differences from other macOS indicators.

Pending, partial or interrupted measurements are identified in the interface. Kaze does not bypass macOS protections. Some folders require Full Disk Access; others may remain inaccessible. Scanning does not guarantee finding all data associated with an app or determining which files matter to you.
